My Five support

My Five shows today’s five salah times and a countdown to the next prayer. Times are calculated on your iPhone. There are no adverts and nothing to buy.

  • Tap the city name to search the shipped worldwide list.
  • Use my location is optional. If you decline, the city picker still works.
  • The small ••• control opens settings for calculation method, Asr madhhab, clock format, and local prayer alerts.
  • Alerts can be switched per prayer and set to fire at the time, 5 minutes before, or 15 minutes before. They stay on this iPhone.
  • Add the Next prayer or Today widget from the iOS widget gallery. Shortcuts can ask for the next prayer or set a local reminder.

Times are an on-device estimate from standard astronomical methods. Local mosque boards may differ by a few minutes. This app is not a fatwa.
