My Five privacy policy

My Five is made by DeskLink.ai. This policy applies to the iOS app (bundle identifier ai.desklink.prayerguide).

The app does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporters, and no third-party SDKs.

Salah times are calculated on your iPhone from a city list shipped inside the app. The app does not call a prayer-times API.

If you tap Use my location, iOS may share your coarse coordinates with the app while it is in use. Those coordinates stay on the device and are used only to calculate today’s times. Location is never uploaded. You can refuse permission and pick a city instead.

If you turn on prayer alerts, iOS local notifications are scheduled on this iPhone for the times you choose. There is no remote push server. Those reminders are not sent to DeskLink.ai.

Home and Lock Screen widgets, and Shortcuts, read the same on-device settings (city, method, and clock format) through an App Group on this iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Settings are stored in on-device UserDefaults.
