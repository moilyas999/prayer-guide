Prayer Guide privacy policy

Prayer Guide is made by DeskLink.ai. This policy applies to the iOS app (bundle identifier ai.desklink.prayerguide).

The app does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporters, and no third-party SDKs.

Salah times are calculated on your iPhone from a city list shipped inside the app. The app does not call a prayer-times API.

If you tap Use my location, iOS may share your coarse coordinates with the app while it is in use. Those coordinates stay on the device and are used only to calculate today’s times. Location is never uploaded. You can refuse permission and pick a city instead.

If you turn on reminders, iOS local notifications fire at prayer time. Those reminders are scheduled on the device and are not sent to DeskLink.ai.

Settings (city, calculation method, clock format, and reminder preference) are stored in on-device UserDefaults.
